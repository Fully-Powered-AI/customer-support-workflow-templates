## The outcome

Every review the business received last month is read, counted and placed, and the whole picture fits on one page. The counts by site and by star rating add up to the export, and a check line at the top proves it. What reviewers keep coming back to is grouped into themes, praise and criticism alike, each with the review IDs behind it and one short line in a reviewer's own words, so anyone can open the export and see the evidence.

If last month's summary is supplied, each site's count and average rating sit beside last month's, and each theme shows last month's count beside this month's, or that it is new or no longer seen. The customer service lead gets the list of reviews still waiting for an answer, oldest first, with how many days each has waited.

Some reviews should never wait in a monthly queue. A review that reports a safety problem, an illness, discrimination or a legal threat is lifted out and set apart for the person you name, before anyone replies to it. Reviewers appear only by their initials, and a member of staff named in a review appears only in a separate note for the manager, never in the summary itself. Nobody is scored or ranked.

## Who starts the work, and when

The owner, the customer service lead or the marketing lead runs it in the first working days of each month, once the reviews export for the month is complete. The person who runs it reads the summary, passes the set-apart reviews to the named person the same day, gives the no-reply list to whoever answers reviews, and hands the manager's note to the manager alone.

The workflow template prepares the summary. It drafts no reply and posts nothing, reports no review to any site, never says whether a review is genuine, and never suggests asking a site to remove one. It needs no connection to any review site or platform.

## What they supply

- **The reviews export from your reviews and reputation platform**, one row per review, with these columns: review ID, date, site, rating, reviewer name, text, replied (yes or no), reply date, and location or product. The workflow template lists the default column headers; if one of yours does not match, it stops and asks which column is which before reading a row.
- **The period** the summary covers.
- **Your theme list,** if you keep one: the things you care about hearing, such as waiting times, product quality or prices. Without one, the workflow template names the themes itself.
- **Who replies to reviews,** who decides on the reviews set apart, and which manager receives the note on staff named in reviews.
- **Last month's summary,** if you want the comparison.

## What comes out

One summary in a fixed order:

1. **Counts by site and rating,** with the average for each site and for all sites, and a check line that ties every count to the export.
2. **Set apart for the named person.** Reviews that report safety, illness, discrimination or a legal threat, each described plainly with no names, and kept out of the themes.
3. **Themes.** Each with whether reviewers praise it, criticise it or both, the review IDs, the shops or products concerned, last month's count, and one short quoted line.
4. **Reviews in no theme,** so nothing read is lost.
5. **What changed since last month,** site by site.
6. **No reply yet, oldest first,** with the reviewer's initials and the days waiting.
7. **Questions** a person needs to settle, such as a review marked as answered with no reply date.

After the closing line comes the manager's note: each review that names a member of staff, with its ID, site and date and the name as written, and nothing else. It is cut off before the summary is shared. A section with nothing in it says "None."

## What has to change for your company

Match the column headers to your export once, and give each review site a plain label. Write down your theme list in your own words, in the order that matters to you. Name who reads the summary, who replies to reviews, who decides on reviews that are set apart, and which manager receives the note on staff. Add any matter you want set apart beyond the four the workflow template already looks for. Decide where the summary is kept, and how the manager's note reaches the manager and nobody else. It runs in your AI assistant from the export you supply.
